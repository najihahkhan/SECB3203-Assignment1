## 3. How we make decisions

We discuss design choices, rule interpretations and task division during our weekly group meeting and on WhatsApp. We aim for consensus first. If we cannot reach an agreement, we hold a majorrity vote and the coordinator breaks any tie. All agreed decisions and task assingmenets are recorded in our pinned WhatsApp group notes and tracked in `docs/design.md` or commit messages.

## 4. Meetings and communication

We meet weekly on Sunday at 8:30pm via Webex or in person on campus. We communicate daily through our group WhatsApp chat. Every member agrees to respond to messages within 12 hours on weekdays and within 24 hours on weekends.

## 5. Rules for Git and for code

- Every members work under their oown name: 'git config user.name' and 'user.email' are set on everyone laptop.
- Every member makes at least 3 commits of real work. Nobody commit for somebody else.
- Commit messages say what changed and why. Small commits are better than big commit.
- Before finalizing changes on 'main', at least one other team member reviews the code and runs 'python -m pytest' and 'ruff check src/' to verify that all tests and lint check pass.

All members acknowledge that the lecturer will use the Git commit history and the individual Q&A to adjust marks if contributions are unequal.
